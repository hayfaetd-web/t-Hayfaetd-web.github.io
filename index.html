Research

One project so far has carried my research: preventing thermal runaway in lithium-ion batteries.

Thermal runaway prevention in lithium-ion batteries

THERMOBATT project · ICube (CNRS / INSA Strasbourg) · Feb 2026 – Jul 2026 · final-year project

  • Developed and validated a 3D electro-thermal model in COMSOL for cylindrical cells with two chemistries, LFP and NMC.
  • Compared simulation with measurements from an instrumented test bench, using infrared thermography to follow how the cells heat up.
  • Simulated critical scenarios and extracted predictive indicators to anticipate thermal runaway.
  • Wrote up the results in two IEEE papers and in my final-year thesis.
Method
3D multiphysics modelling, finite-element methods, correlation of calculation and measurement
Data
Test bench measurements and infrared thermal images
Tools
COMSOL Multiphysics
Why it matters for AI
Early warning of abnormal thermal behaviour is an anomaly detection problem.
